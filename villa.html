☆ More than just a retreat ☆

The luxury of earned silence

Formerly known as Tarika Residences, Villa Anvira by GTV Estate is an elegant 7-bedroom luxury villa in Chail. Set amidst whispering pine trees and overlooking the tranquil valleys of Himachal, this private mountain retreat is thoughtfully crafted for families and large groups of friends seeking space and serenity.

Each bedroom opens to a private balcony with sweeping forest or valley views, welcoming sunlit mornings, and refreshing mountain winds. Guests can spend their days exploring scenic trails around the estate and their evenings indulging in barbecue feasts and cosy bonfires under the stars. A charming gazebo, high-speed Wi-Fi, and freshly prepared meals on request elevate both leisurely escapes and peaceful workcations.

Located near Shimla's popular attractions yet peacefully tucked away from the crowds, Villa Anvira by GTV Estate offers quiet luxury, natural beauty, and togetherness at the heart of the Himalayas.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum length of stay?

Villa AnVira requires a minimum stay of 2 nights.

Is the villa pet-friendly?

Yes — Villa AnVira is pet-friendly. Let us know ahead of arrival so the staff can prepare accordingly.

What's included, and what costs extra?

Included: caretaker & staff on site, daily housekeeping, Wi-Fi & parking, and morning tea service. Available on request (paid separately): chef-prepared meals, bonfire evenings, barbecue setup, and local excursions.

Is there parking, and what time can we arrive?

Free on-site parking is available for guests. Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM (per our Terms of Stay); directions and caretaker contact details are shared in your private pre-arrival guide once a stay is confirmed.
